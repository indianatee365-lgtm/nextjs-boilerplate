# Twilio A2P 10DLC Campaign Details

## Campaign Description

When creating an account on tee365.org, customers provide their phone number and check a required SMS consent checkbox that reads: "I agree to receive transactional SMS messages from Tee365 (booking confirmations and access codes). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for info." Account creation is not possible without checking this box. Tee365 sends transactional SMS messages to these customers including booking confirmations and time-sensitive facility access codes required to enter the building. Opt-out: Reply STOP. Help: Reply HELP or email info@tee365.org.
