# Twilio A2P 10DLC Campaign Details

## Campaign Description

Tee365 is an indoor golf simulator facility in South Bend, Indiana. We send transactional SMS messages to customers who have explicitly consented during account creation. Messages include booking confirmations (sent immediately after payment) and facility access codes (sent 10–20 minutes before a session begins). No marketing or promotional messages are sent. Opt-out: Reply STOP. Help: Reply HELP or email info@tee365.org.

## Opt-In / Call to Action (CTA) Description

Customers provide their phone number and check a **required** SMS consent checkbox during account creation at **https://tee365.org/signup**. The checkbox text reads verbatim:

> "I agree to receive transactional SMS messages from Tee365 (booking confirmations and access codes). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for info."

Account creation is not possible without checking this box — the server rejects form submissions where consent is absent. Consent is recorded at the time of account creation and linked to the customer's phone number.

Full opt-in policy including consent language, message types, sample messages, and opt-out instructions is publicly documented at: **https://tee365.org/sms-opt-in**
