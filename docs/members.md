# Tee365 Membership Programs
**Complete Reference Document**
*Last Updated: April 2026*

---

## Overview

Tee365 offers three membership tiers designed for different types of players. Each tier has a clear reason to exist and a distinct audience. No tier undercuts another in a way that makes members feel cheated.

---

## Tier 1: Birdie Membership

**Who it's for:** The casual player. Someone who comes in once or twice a month and wants a small discount and a reason to feel like a regular without a major financial commitment.

**Monthly fee:** $10/mo
**Joining fee:** None
**Availability:** Always open enrollment

### Benefits
- 10% off rack rate on all bay time
- 10-day advance booking window (vs. 7 days for non-members)
- Maximum of 2 active reservations at a time (rolling)
- Access to member-only communications and updates

### Break-Even
At a blended $40/hr rack rate, the 10% discount saves $4/hr. The membership pays for itself in under 3 sessions per month.

### Annual Option: Birdie Season Pass
- **Price:** $89/year (saves $31 vs monthly)
- Annual period begins September 1, 2026 for pre-launch purchases regardless of purchase date
- No joining fee, no signup bonus

---

## Tier 2: Eagle Membership

**Who it's for:** The frequent or serious player. Someone playing 4 or more times per month who wants priority access, a meaningful discount, and a signup bonus that covers the first visit.

**Monthly fee:** $39/mo
**Joining fee:** None
**Availability:** Always open enrollment

### Benefits
- 20% off rack rate on all bay time
- 14-day advance booking window (vs. 7 days for non-members)
- Maximum of 3 active reservations at a time (rolling)
- 2 free hours at signup (one-time, non-transferable, expires 90 days after signup)
- Early registration for leagues and events
- Access to member-only communications and updates

### Break-Even
At a blended $45/hr rack rate, the 20% discount saves $9/hr. The membership pays for itself in just over 4 sessions per month, which is the floor of the target usage for this tier. The 2-hour signup bonus alone covers the first month's fee at most rack rates.

### Annual Option: Eagle Season Pass
- **Price:** $349/year (saves $119 vs monthly)
- Annual period begins September 1, 2026 for pre-launch purchases regardless of purchase date
- 2-hour signup bonus still applies

## Tier 3: Founder's Club

**Who it's for:** The early believers. The 100 people who backed Tee365 before the doors opened. This tier is permanently unavailable after the founding period closes.

**Monthly fee:** $29/mo
**Joining fee:** $199 one-time (collected at signup)
**Availability:** Limited to 100 memberships ever. Sales close permanently on August 18, 2026 (two weeks before opening day) or when the cap is reached, whichever comes first.

### Benefits
- 20% off rack rate on all bay time, forever (floor of $20/hr)
- Year-one bonus: 30% total discount for the first 12 months post-opening (September 1, 2026 through August 31, 2027)
- 21-day advance booking window (best available)
- Maximum of 3 active reservations at a time (rolling)
- 2 free hours at Founders and Friends Day (pre-opening event on August 31, 2026, one day before opening, for Founders, friends, and family)
- Early league registration plus reserved slot guarantee
- Name on the permanent Founders Wall in the facility
- Permanent member number (#1 through #100)
- 48-hour first access to the booking calendar before public opening
- Access to private Founder's update channel during pre-opening period
- Access to member-only communications and updates

### Break-Even on Joining Fee
At 30% off during year one, a Founder playing 4 hours per month at $50 peak saves $60/month in bay time. The $199 joining fee is recovered in under 4 months of regular use. After that, savings compound for the life of the membership.

### No Annual Option for Founders
Founders are already making a long-term commitment. No annual prepay option is offered or needed for this tier.

## Cancellation and Refund Policy

### Monthly Memberships (Birdie and Eagle)

- Cancel anytime. No cancellation fee.
- Benefits remain active through the end of the current billing period.
- No refund for partial months.

### Annual Season Passes (Birdie and Eagle)

- Within 30 days of purchase: full refund, no questions asked.
- After 30 days: prorated refund of unused months minus a $25 processing fee.
- After 9 months of a 12-month pass: no refund. Remaining value is considered used.
- Hardship pause available once per year for up to 90 days. Annual end date extends accordingly. Medical documentation may be requested.

### Founder's Club

- The $199 joining fee is non-refundable after 30 days.
- Within 30 days of joining fee payment: full refund of joining fee plus any monthly fees collected.
- Monthly fees: same cancellation terms as monthly memberships above.
- If Tee365 does not open within 60 days of the stated September 1, 2026 opening date, all Founder fees (joining fee plus monthly fees) are fully refundable upon request.

## Founder's Club Status Policy

Founder status is permanent and irrevocable. A Founder who cancels their monthly membership retains their member number and their name on the Founders Wall. Active benefits (discount, booking window, reservation priority, league access) are tied to an active monthly membership and pause upon cancellation.

**Reactivation:** A Founder who cancels and later reactivates does so at their original Founder terms, not at current market rates. This option is available indefinitely.

**Reactivation limit:** If a Founder cancels and reactivates more than three times, Tee365 reserves the right to convert the membership to standard Eagle terms. This policy exists to prevent abuse of the reactivation benefit and will be applied at Tee365's discretion.

**Relocation:** A Founder who moves out of the area retains full status. Monthly membership may be paused (not refunded) for up to 6 months with advance notice. Benefits resume upon reactivation.

**Death:** Founder status is non-transferable. The Founder's name remains on the wall permanently. The estate may request a prorated refund of any prepaid fees.

**Transfers:** Founder memberships are non-transferable under any circumstances.

## Operational Rules

### Booking Windows by Tier

| Tier | Advance Booking |
|---|---|
| Walk-in / Non-member | Up to 7 days in advance |
| Birdie | Up to 10 days in advance |
| Eagle | Up to 14 days in advance |
| Founder | Up to 21 days in advance |

### Active Reservation Caps

| Tier | Max Active Reservations |
|---|---|
| Birdie | 2 at a time (rolling) |
| Eagle | 3 at a time (rolling) |
| Founder | 3 at a time (rolling) |

Rolling means: once a reservation is completed or cancelled, a new one can be made immediately up to the cap.

### Discount Application

Discounts apply to bay time only. They do not apply to league fees, retail purchases, gift cards, or any future add-on services unless explicitly stated.

The Founder discount floor of $20/hr applies regardless of the scheduled rack rate. At the lowest off-season rack rate of $25/hr, Founders pay $20/hr, always saving something on every session.

### League and Event Priority

Founders receive early registration access plus a reserved slot guarantee for any Tee365 league or event. Eagle members receive early registration access without the reserved slot guarantee. Birdie members and non-members register at standard open enrollment.

## Changes to Benefits

Tee365 reserves the right to modify membership benefits with 30 days written notice to affected members. Founder discount rates and booking window advantages are the sole benefits exempt from unilateral reduction. All other benefits may be adjusted as the business requires.
